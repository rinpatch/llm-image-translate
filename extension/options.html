Image Translate

Two OpenAI-compatible endpoints: one finds and reads the text, one translates it. They can be the same server.

Profile

A named pair of endpoints, switchable from the popup. The one picked here is the one in use and the one the fields below edit.

Reader

Locates the text in the image and says what it says.

Translator

Translates what the reader found.

Layout

A vertical Japanese column is about one glyph wide and no font size makes it hold a line of English, so a flood fill looks for the speech balloon it sits in and lays the text out in there instead. Off, translations stay in the box the reader drew — the shape to grow into is a comic's, and a screenshot or a photo has none.

Pages

The badge appears while ⌘ (Super on Windows and Linux) is held, and stays out once an image has been translated so you can put the original back.
