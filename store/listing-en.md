LLM Image Translate: Manga, Webtoon & Image Translator

## Summary (max 132)

Translate text inside images on any site with your ChatGPT plan, free OpenRouter models, Chrome's translator, or your own server.

## Description

Translate the text inside images, right on the page. Manga, webtoons, posts on X, Pixiv art, memes, menus, shop pages: the original words are painted out and the translation is lettered in their place. The result is a real image you can save.

USE WHAT YOU ALREADY HAVE
• Your ChatGPT plan: sign in with ChatGPT and translate on your Plus, Pro, Business, Edu or Enterprise plan. No credits, no extra subscription.
• OpenRouter: pick one of its free models, or a translation model that costs a fraction of a cent per image.
• Chrome's built-in translator: free, nothing to set up.
• Any OpenAI-compatible server: llama.cpp, Ollama, LM Studio, or your own.

WORKS WHERE YOU READ
• X (Twitter), Pixiv, Naver Webtoon, and any other site.
• Turn on auto-translate for a site and images are translated before you scroll to them.
• Hold ⌘ (the Windows or Super key elsewhere) to show a badge on any image, and click it to translate. Click again for the original.

MADE FOR COMICS AND ART
• Reads Japanese, Chinese, Korean and English, set vertically or horizontally.
• Removes the original lettering with an inpainting model trained on manga, so speech bubbles and backgrounds stay clean.
• Translates into 20 languages, including English, Russian, Indonesian, Chinese, Spanish and Portuguese.

NO ACCOUNT NECESSARY
• The text is read on your computer, inside the browser.
• Images never leave your computer: only the words it read go to the translator you picked.
• No sign-up, no tracking, no ads.
• Open source under GPL-3.0: https://github.com/rinpatch/llm-image-translate
